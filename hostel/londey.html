How It Works

  1. Schedule Pickup via the Hostel App or Reception
  2. Bag Your Clothes – Use the laundry bag provided
  3. We Clean – Professionally washed, dried, and folded
  4. Delivery Back to You – Within 24–48 hours

Guidelines

  • Label your laundry bag with your room number.
  • Report missing/delayed laundry within 24 hours.
  • Avoid including delicate or dry-clean-only items.
